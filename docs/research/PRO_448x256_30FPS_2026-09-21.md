# 30 FPS reached at 448×256 internal resolution — 31.91 / 32.13 FPS

**Audience:** the engineer deciding whether to ship this, and the one continuing the work.
**Date:** 2026-09-21. **Supersedes** the "30 FPS is unreachable" conclusion in
`PRO_PHASE123_RESULTS_2026-09-21.md` with the one qualification that conclusion carried:
it is unreachable **at 576×320**. This is the resolution path.

## Result

| Arm | Resolution | FPS | dit | vae_decode | motion_enc | peak VRAM | reserved |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| control (full stack) | 576×320 | 18.9023 | 3.578 | 8.134 | 1.117 | 9,391 | — |
| **lowres-full** | **448×256** | **31.9079** | 2.207 | 4.627 | 0.706 | 10,603 | 9,892 |

Artifacts: `benchmarks/pro_30fps_20260921/lowres-full/results.json`, `lowres-fast/results.json`.

**+69% over the full-resolution build, +99% over the 16.03 FPS the session began at.** The
decoder — the binding constraint at 576×320, at its FP16 floor — fell **8.134 → 4.627 s**
(−43%), tracking the 0.622 area factor almost exactly (0.622 × 8.134 = 5.06 predicted;
the extra came from smaller activations improving the engines' cache behaviour). DiT fell
38% on 4,032 vs 6,480 tokens. This is the lever the arithmetic said would work, working.

## What was built

- `--resolution H W` flag on the harness (multiples of 16; latents must be even for the
  2× patchify). Wired to the profile and to `prepare_params`, which resizes the reference.
- `--no-capture-linears` flag: the default capture path records every DiT projection
  (3.8 GB for one block alone) and blew its budget. Decoder-input latents are all a stage
  calibration needs.
- New capture → calibration → build cycle at 448×256:
  `lowres-capture/` → `stage-calibration-448x256/` → `stage-fp16-448x256-build-r01/`.
  9 signatures, preflight PASS, shared arena **882.9 MiB** (vs 1,418.9 at 576×320).
- Policy `policies/lowres_full.json` pointing at the new plan.
- `review.py --allow-resolution-drift`, plus its side-by-side comparison now scales the
  candidate to the baseline before `hstack` (which requires equal heights and was failing
  after writing nothing).

The stage tooling needed no change: every shape derives from `tensor_signature` and
`value.shape`; nothing hardcodes 576×320.

## Quality — the thing to decide on

Numeric gate (`review-lowres/review.json`, control at 576×320 vs candidate at 448×256):

| metric | value | read as |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `opening_correlation` | **0.9385 at lag 0** | lip motion tracks the control; no shift (best lag is 0) |
| `median_edge_ratio_on_both_open` | 1.0700 | **resolution-biased** — raw edge energy at a smaller scale; a flag, not a verdict |
| `median_mouth_center_distance_px` | 61.4 | **coordinate mismatch** between 448×256 and 576×320 frames; not a displacement |
| per-window frame count | `[33]×9`, uniform | the geometry assertion holds |

Visual (`review-lowres/mouth-comparison.png`, and the still built from `raw.npy`):
lip shapes track closely; **teeth are present but visibly softer**, with grey speckle at
frames 12 and 62; the whole frame is softer after Lanczos upscale to 576×320. This is the
"slightly degraded" category the user authorised, not the "broken" category of the
rejected 1-step arm (which had speckled grey-green teeth throughout and magenta
oversaturation).

**Videos for the decision**, candidate upscaled to 576×320 for a like-for-like view:
`benchmarks/pro_30fps_20260921/visual/lowres-threeway.mp4` and `lowres-mouth-threeway.mp4`.

## Rollback

One flag. Omit `--resolution` and use `policies/phase1.json` and the run is back at
576×320 / 18.90 FPS with the original engines untouched. Nothing in `vae.py` changed, so
the 576×320 engines remain valid.

## Not done / open

- The static INT8 activation scales (`int8-amax.json`) were calibrated at 6,480 tokens
  and were **not** applied here; the DiT ran with dynamic per-row scales. Recalibrating at
  448×256 would recover an estimated ~20 ms/window `[E]`, scaled from the 576×320
  measurement. Unmeasured.
- Aggregate/concurrency: one instance still peaks at 10.6 GB. The smaller arena helps
  (−536 MiB) but a second instance does not fit. Reaching 30 FPS single-stream does not
  by itself change that.
